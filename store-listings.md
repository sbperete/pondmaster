## Long Description (4000 chars — Google Play)

**PondMaster — Professional Tilapia Pond Management**

Built for small-scale fish farmers across southern Africa, PondMaster gives you the tools to run your tilapia operation like a professional — from fingerling stocking to final harvest.

**🐟 Pond Tracking**
Manage up to 3 ponds on the free plan. Track fingerlings stocked, water source, aerator type, liner, and every daily log entry from feeding to water quality.

**📊 Growth Intelligence**
PondMaster uses a proven Nile tilapia growth curve (fingerling → 700g over 28 weeks) to estimate current fish weight, calculate daily feed amounts, and project your harvest revenue at months 5, 6, and 7.

**⚖️ Daily Log**
Log feed type, amount, water temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH, and mortality counts in under 60 seconds. Weigh 10 fish weekly — the app updates your growth estimate automatically.

**🏥 Health Events**
Record disease outbreaks, water quality emergencies, and treatment outcomes. Get fast answers from the built-in disease reference guide covering ich, columnaris, oxygen crashes, and more.

**📅 Calendar**
See all upcoming events at a glance — harvest targets, weekly weigh days, water change reminders, and feed phase transitions — all auto-generated from your stocking dates.

**🤖 AI Advisor**
Ask your AI advisor anything about tilapia farming. Bring your own API key (Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or Gemini). The AI gets full context about your ponds, current growth stage, and recent logs.

**👷 Worker Card**
Generate a print-ready worker card for your pond workers — shows today's feed amounts in English and Chichewa, no login required. Valid for 7 days.

**🌐 Works Offline**
PondMaster works on 2G and offline. Daily logs are saved locally and synced to the cloud when you reconnect. Built as a Progressive Web App — install it on your phone's home screen.

**🌙 Dark Mode**
Full dark and light themes. Beautiful on any device.

**Languages**: English + Chichewa
**Free plan**: 3 ponds, unlimited logs
**No subscription**: core features are free forever
