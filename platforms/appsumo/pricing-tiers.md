## WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED IN ANY TIER

- **AI API costs** — The AI Farming Assistant uses your own API key. You pay your AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, or Google) directly. Typical usage is under $1/month for a single farm.
- **Supabase storage overages** — Extremely large photo attachments (if added in a future update) may incur storage costs. Standard text-and-number farm records do not approach any limit.
